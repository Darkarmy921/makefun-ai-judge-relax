The evening light in this room is the colour of weak tea.

Outside, the street is emptying itself, one shopfront at a time, and the last bus goes past without stopping.

The kettle boiled an hour ago. Nobody has poured from it since.

A dog sleeps by the door, its paws twitching at a dream it will not remember.

You have read so many of these. This one asks nothing of you, not even a verdict — the next pair will still be there when you are ready.

For now, the light stays where it is, thin and gold on the floorboards, and it will still be there in an hour.
